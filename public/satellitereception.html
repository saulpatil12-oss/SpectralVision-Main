Satellite Reception Guide – GK-2A

Receive GK-2A meteorological imagery at home using SDR or dish antennas

A) Introduction to GK-2A

  • Satellite: GK-2A, a geostationary meteorological satellite over Asia.
  • Transmissions: HRIT (High-Rate Information Transmission) and LRIT (Low-Rate Information Transmission).
  • Home Reception: Signal in L-band (1691.0–1692.5 MHz) is receivable with consumer SDRs and small antennas due to strong GEO broadcast power.

C) Setup Method 1: RTL-SDR + Bi-pole Antenna

  • Connection Chain: Antenna → LNA → L-band Filter → RTL-SDR → PC
  • Positioning: Place antenna outdoors with clear line of sight to GEO satellite at ~128°E longitude.
  • Typical SNR: 10–18 dB indoors, 15–25 dB outdoors

D) Setup Method 2: Dish Antenna + LNB

  • Feed Mount: LNB should face the dish focus precisely.
  • Dish Alignment: Azimuth: ~128°E, Elevation: 30–40° depending on location in India.
  • Suggested Dish Size: 60–90 cm for most Indian cities

E) Software Setup

  • SDR# Settings: Frequency: 1692.14 MHz, Sample Rate: 2.048 MSPS, Gain: Auto, Bandwidth: 2 MHz
  • Decoding: SatDump or LeanHRIT for LRIT/HRIT extraction
  • Post-processing: Use XRIT-Decompress to reconstruct full images

F) Troubleshooting

  • Check cable loss, antenna orientation, and LNA connection
  • Verify dish azimuth & elevation
  • Move setup outdoors if interference occurs
